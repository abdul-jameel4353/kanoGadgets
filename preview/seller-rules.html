Seller Rules & Regulations

Every seller must read and agree to these rules before publishing.

  1. Only people who genuinely work in Kano's Gadget Center may register and sell on the platform.
  2. Sellers must provide truthful identity and shop information.
  3. Product names, descriptions, prices, photographs and condition must be accurate.
  4. Counterfeit, stolen, fraudulent, prohibited or otherwise unlawful products are not allowed.
  5. Sellers must provide a valid WhatsApp contact number.
  6. Sellers must communicate honestly and professionally with customers.
  7. Fake listings, scams and repeated misleading conduct may result in suspension.
  8. Administrators may remove harmful, fraudulent, misleading or prohibited listings.
  9. Sellers must respect customers' privacy and handle their personal information responsibly.
  10. Sellers must not misuse the marketplace or attempt to access another seller's account.
  11. Sellers must comply with applicable laws and the marketplace's published policies.
  12. The marketplace may suspend or terminate accounts for serious or repeated violations.
