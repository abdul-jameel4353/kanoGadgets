Terms of Use

Welcome to KanoPlug.com. These terms explain how the marketplace works and what users must do.

What the marketplace is

KanoPlug.com is a platform that helps customers discover gadgets offered by registered sellers associated with Kano's Gadget Center. We connect buyers and sellers; we do not process payments, guarantees or shipment.

Seller responsibilities

  • Publish only genuine, accurately described, lawfully sellable products.
  • Use real photographs and correct prices.
  • Respond honestly to customer inquiries through WhatsApp.
  • Handle customer personal information responsibly.

Customer responsibilities

  • Verify the product and the seller before completing a purchase.
  • Meet in safe, public places where reasonable.
  • Report suspicious listings or conduct.

Moderation

The marketplace owner may suspend seller accounts at any time, and may remove or restrict listings that are harmful, fraudulent, misleading or prohibited. KanoPlug.com may suspend accounts for repeated or serious violations.

KanoPlug.com is a discovery and communication platform. Transactions are handled directly between customer and seller.
