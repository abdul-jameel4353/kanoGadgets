Privacy Policy

KanoGadgets.com is a marketplace where sellers list gadgets and customers contact them directly. We collect the minimum personal information necessary to run the marketplace and keep it safe.

What we collect

  • Account email and password (hashed) for identified users and sellers.
  • Product and shop information that sellers publish.
  • Application details from sellers, including a passport photograph used only for verification.
  • Reports submitted by visitors about suspicious listings.

How we use it

  • To manage accounts and the marketplace.
  • To review seller applications and verify businesses.
  • To investigate reports of scams, counterfeit goods or abusive conduct.
  • To communicate with users and sellers.

Access and protection

Passport photographs are kept private and are visible only to the marketplace owner and authorised reviewers. We do not publish application photos in listings, profiles, sitemaps or public URLs.

Retention

Application records are kept only as long as needed to manage seller verification, and are securely disposed of when no longer required, subject to legitimate record-keeping.

Contact

To ask questions about how KanoGadgets.com handles personal information, contact hello@kanogadgets.com.
