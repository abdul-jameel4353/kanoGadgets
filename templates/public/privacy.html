Privacy Policy

KanoPlug.com is a marketplace where sellers list gadgets and customers contact them directly. We collect the minimum personal information necessary to run the marketplace and keep it safe.

What we collect

  • Account email and password (hashed), name, username and WhatsApp number for sellers.
  • Product and shop information that sellers publish.
  • Reports submitted by visitors about suspicious listings.

How we use it

  • To manage accounts and the marketplace.
  • To investigate reports of scams, counterfeit goods or abusive conduct.
  • To communicate with users and sellers.

Access and protection

Seller emails and phone details are visible to the marketplace owner for moderation and safety purposes. Passwords are stored only as bcrypt hashes — we can never see your password.

Retention

Account records are kept only as long as needed to run the marketplace, and are securely disposed of when no longer required, subject to legitimate record-keeping.

Contact

To ask questions about how KanoPlug.com handles personal information, contact hello@kanoplug.com.
